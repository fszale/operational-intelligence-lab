# Phase 2: Use Case Selection

> **Duration**: Weeks 3–4 | **Your time commitment**: ~2 hours (one alignment meeting)

## What Happens

This is the most important decision in the entire pilot: **which metric do we go after?**

Your Fellow will present the findings from the Discovery phase and recommend 2–3 AI opportunities. Together, you'll pick the one that matters most to your business.

### The Alignment Meeting

This is a structured 60–90 minute meeting between you, your Fellow, and (optionally) your key team members.

**Agenda:**

| Time | Topic | What You Do |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 10 min | Process mapping review | Confirm accuracy |
| 15 min | Top 3 opportunities presented | Listen, ask questions |
| 10 min | ROI estimates (conservative) | See projected value |
| 15 min | Metric selection | **You decide what matters** |
| 15 min | Baseline measurement plan | Agree on how to measure |
| 10 min | Next steps + timeline | Confirm commitment |

### Choosing the Right Metric

The best metric to target is one that:

✅ **You already care about** — Don't let us pick something "interesting." Pick something that keeps you up at night.

✅ **Is measurable today** — We need a number, not a feeling. Hours, dollars, errors, response times.

✅ **Has clear business impact** — If this number improves, your bottom line or your team's sanity improves too.

✅ **Is influenced by the process we audited** — The AI workflow needs to be able to move this needle.

### What the Fellow Presents

For each opportunity, your Fellow will show:

```mermaid
graph LR
    A["🔍 The Problem"] --> B["💡 Proposed Solution"]
    B --> C["📊 Expected ROI"]
    C --> D["⚠️ Risk Level"]
    D --> E["⏱️ Time to Deploy"]

    style A fill:#1a1a2e,stroke:#e94560,color:#fff
    style C fill:#0f3460,stroke:#e94560,color:#fff
    style E fill:#2d6a4f,stroke:#40916c,color:#fff
```

- **The problem**: What's happening today and what it costs you
- **Proposed solution**: What the AI workflow would do (in plain language)
- **Expected ROI**: Conservative estimate of value in the first 12 weeks
- **Risk level**: What could go wrong and how we'd handle it
- **Time to deploy**: How quickly we can get this running

## Your Decision

After the meeting, you'll confirm:

1. **The metric** you want to improve
2. **The baseline** — what the number is right now
3. **The target** — what "good" looks like (optional, but helpful)
4. **Staff involvement** — who on your team will work with the Fellow
5. **Data access** — what the Fellow needs to build the workflow

## What Happens Next

Once you agree on the use case, the Fellow moves into [Phase 3: Implementation](phase-3-implementation.md). You'll see the first working AI workflow within 2 weeks.
